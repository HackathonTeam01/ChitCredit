# Chit Credit Pitch Deck

## Slide 1 - The income is real. The credit file is missing.

A delivery worker can earn ₹200 one day and ₹1,200 the next. The work is real; conventional credit records often miss the pattern.

**Speaker notes:** Chit payments are a behavioral signal that already exists. We make it legible without pretending income is fixed.

## Slide 2 - Consistency beats a snapshot

- Daily income is volatile.
- Contribution behavior accumulates over time.
- A fair signal should explain how it was earned.

**Speaker notes:** Chit Credit combines payment reliability, streak, tenure, and missed payments into an explainable score.

## Slide 3 - Three layers, one loop

1. Ledger and score create the signal.
2. Income smoothing builds a reserve toward the fixed due.
3. Elastic repayment adjusts to today's earnings.

**Speaker notes:** The system protects both sides of the circle: operators see risk clearly, while members avoid a rigid deduction on a slow day.

## Slide 4 - Live demo: from jagged income to a paid contribution

- Open the member dashboard.
- Show the smoothing wallet target.
- Confirm a contribution.
- Show the API-backed auto-payment notification.

**Speaker notes:** The notification is emitted from the contribution response, not from a timer.

## Slide 5 - Real-world anchor

- India has a legal framework for chit funds under the Chit Funds Act, 1982.
- Accion's CredRight profile is a relevant precedent for digitizing chit-fund member access to formal finance.
- IppoPay is a payments-context reference, not a claimed project partner.

**Speaker notes:** Use only the claims and links in `REAL_WORLD_FACTS.md`. Do not improvise a subscriber number without verifying it first.

## Slide 6 - A healthier circle compounds

- Better contribution visibility.
- More predictable group liquidity.
- Credit offers sized from explainable behavior.
- Repayments that respond to earning capacity.

**Speaker notes:** This is responsible access, not a promise of guaranteed approval.

## Slide 7 - Make the work visible

Chit Credit turns daily income volatility into an explainable path to reliable chit payments and fair formal credit.

**Speaker notes:** Close on the member's real behavior, not on a speculative market-size claim.
