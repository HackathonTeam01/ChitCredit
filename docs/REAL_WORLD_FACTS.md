# Chit Credit Real-World Fact Pack

This pack deliberately separates verified source-backed claims from product assumptions. Do not present a numeric market claim on stage unless the team has rechecked the linked source immediately before the pitch.

## CredRight

Accion describes CredRight as a financial technology company working with chit funds and their members to improve access to formal financial services. This is the closest source-backed precedent for Chit Credit's thesis: digitized contribution behavior can become a useful financial signal.

Source: [Accion - CredRight](https://www.accion.org/credright)

## Chit funds in India

The legal framework for chit funds is established by the Chit Funds Act, 1982. The act provides the correct regulatory anchor for the pitch, but it is not itself a current subscriber-count statistic. We should not claim a number of subscribers without a current Ministry of Finance, RBI, or Registrar of Chits source.

Source: [India Code - The Chit Funds Act, 1982](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/1597)

## IppoPay

IppoPay's official site is the primary source to use when describing its payments context. The pitch should say that Chit Credit is designed to fit QR/UPI collection and informal-worker payment flows; it should not claim a formal IppoPay partnership unless the team has one.

Source: [IppoPay](https://www.ipopay.com/)

## Safe pitch wording

"Chit funds already record a member's payment behavior. Chit Credit turns that behavior, together with variable daily earnings, into an explainable score, a reserve plan, and a repayment amount that responds to the day."

## Claims to verify before presenting

- A current, official count of registered chit-fund subscribers in India.
- Any statement that IppoPay specifically serves chit-fund operators in Tamil Nadu.
- Any partnership, integration, or customer relationship between this project and IppoPay or CredRight.
