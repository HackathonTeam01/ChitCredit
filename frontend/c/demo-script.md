# Chit Credit Demo Script

## 0:00-0:40 | Hook
"A reliable informal worker can earn every day and still be invisible to formal credit because income does not arrive on a fixed salary date. Chit Credit starts with behavior the community already trusts: contributions made over time."

## 0:40-1:30 | Layer 1: Ledger and score
1. Open the member workspace.
2. Point to the score, tier, wallet balance, and contribution history.
3. Say: "This score is explainable. It reflects payment reliability and participation, not a black-box prediction."

## 1:30-2:30 | Layer 2: Income smoothing
1. Point to the jagged daily earnings heat map.
2. Adjust the reserve percentage.
3. Show the wallet balance moving toward the Rs 500 target.
4. Say: "On stronger days, the reserve grows faster. On weaker days, the member is protected. When the target is reached, the contribution can be auto-paid on time."

## 2:30-3:15 | Layer 3: Elastic repayment
1. Move the Today's Earnings slider from a strong day to a slow day.
2. Point to the deduction changing with earnings.
3. Say: "Repayment follows capacity. A lower-income day does not force a fixed installment that breaks the member's cash flow."

## 3:15-3:45 | Real-world anchor
"This fits existing UPI activity and chit-group trust. The product digitizes the behavior already happening, then makes it useful to members, operators, and responsible credit partners."

## 3:45-4:15 | Impact and close
"Members build a financial record, operators see group health earlier, and credit partners get a clearer signal. When daily work is visible, reliable people should not have to remain financially invisible."

## Rehearsal checklist

- Confirm the app opens from a clean browser.
- Keep the member ID and demo values visible before starting.
- Pause after each layer so judges can see the state change.
- If live integration fails, use the backup screenshots and keep the same narrative.
- Target total runtime: 4 minutes 15 seconds.
