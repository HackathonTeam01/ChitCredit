# Chit Credit Pitch Deck

## 1. The invisible credit gap
**Headline:** Good earners are often invisible to formal credit.

- Gig workers and informal merchants earn every day, but their financial story is fragmented.
- A missed payment can look like risk when the real problem is irregular cash flow.
- Chit groups already create trust; the opportunity is to make that trust legible.

**Speaker note:** Start with the person, not the product. Ask judges to imagine a reliable earner whose income does not arrive on a salary date.

## 2. The insight
**Headline:** Repayment behavior is a stronger signal than a payslip alone.

- Chit contributions create a repeated, community-backed record.
- Daily earnings reveal capacity, while on-time contributions reveal reliability.
- Together they form an explainable alternative credit signal.

**Speaker note:** Emphasize explainability: every score can be traced to visible behavior.

## 3. The solution
**Headline:** Chit Credit turns daily work into a usable financial story.

- **Ledger:** digitized contributions and payment history.
- **Smoothing:** a reserve wallet converts uneven earnings into a predictable contribution.
- **Credit:** a transparent score unlocks appropriately sized offers and elastic repayment.

**Speaker note:** This is the three-layer diagram: record, stabilize, extend.

## 4. The live demo
**Headline:** Watch an irregular earning pattern become a reliable reserve.

- Open the member workspace and show the daily earnings heat map.
- Adjust the reserve percentage and show the wallet building toward the contribution target.
- Move to the repayment simulator and show deductions shrink with lower earnings.

**Speaker note:** Keep the explanation tied to the screen. Let the chart do the convincing.

## 5. The real-world anchor
**Headline:** The product fits the rails people already use.

- QR and UPI payments create digital activity for informal merchants.
- Chit operators already coordinate recurring contributions and community trust.
- Chit Credit adds a transparent intelligence layer without asking users to become financial analysts.

**Speaker note:** Insert only verified partner and market statistics before presenting. Do not use an unverified number.

## 6. Impact and sustainability
**Headline:** Reliability becomes visible, and resilience becomes affordable.

- Members build a usable record without needing a conventional payslip.
- Operators get earlier visibility into risk and group liquidity.
- Credit partners receive a clearer signal while repayment stays proportional to income.

**Speaker note:** Connect member benefit, operator benefit, and partner sustainability in that order.

## 7. Close
**Headline:** Chit Credit makes everyday reliability count.

- It starts with the contribution a member already understands.
- It turns uneven income into a steady reserve.
- It gives the next financial decision a better signal.

**Speaker note:** Closing line: "When daily work is visible, reliable people should not have to remain financially invisible."
